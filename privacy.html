Privacy Policy for Checkpoint X-Ray

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

Checkpoint X-Ray does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress, settings, threat gallery and nameplate are saved only in your device's local storage so the game can remember them. It never leaves your device unless you export a save file yourself. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it.

Third-party services

The game loads its fonts from Google Fonts when you are online. Google may receive your IP address as part of that request, as described in the Google Privacy Policy. The game works without them using a fallback font.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
