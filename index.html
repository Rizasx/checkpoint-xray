Threat guide

Items fill in as you scan them. The colours show how the X-ray sees each one.

Rules of the line

Reject

  • Handguns, revolvers, stun guns, knuckle dusters and ammunition. This includes 3D-printed guns.
  • Knives, folding knives, machetes, ceramic knives, box cutters and scissors
  • Grenades, bombs, pipe bombs and fireworks
  • Narcotics (dense organic bricks)
  • Large liquid bottles, aerosol cans and pepper spray. Travel-size bottles are fine.
  • Shielded pouches with no green dots. The pouch hides what is inside.

Clear

  • Laptops, phones, cameras, headphones
  • Hair dryers, razors, power banks, chargers, drones
  • Wrenches, pliers, binoculars, tripods, cutlery, empty flasks
  • Umbrellas, keys, clothes, food, toys and water pistols
  • Film pouches with two green dots

Reading the screen

  • Orange is organic, green is plastic and liquid, blue is metal. Overlaps turn darker.
  • Threats hide under bulky items and inside books, laptops, shoes, teddy bears and umbrellas. Check both views and the filters.
  • Some shifts have an event: rush hour, heightened alert, an inspector, holiday crowds.
  • Terrorists carry bombs and guns. Smugglers carry narcotics. Criminals carry blades and weapons. Catching them pays extra.
  • A wanted notice goes up each shift. If that person walks up, reject the bag even when it is clean.
  • After an arrest you may get to interrogate the suspect. Read their body language, pick an approach, and a confession pays cash and tip-offs about later bags. Pushing too hard gets you a complaint.
  • Passengers may slip you a bribe. Taking it is not an accident. Internal Affairs keeps count, and three strikes means arrest.
  • Click an item on the scan to tag it. Reject a threat bag with the real threat tagged for a 25% pinpoint bonus.
  • Pick a contract at the briefing. Faster lanes and riskier shifts pay more, and so do the harder difficulties.
  • Shifts are rated up to three stars for accuracy and breaches. Three stars pay a bonus.
  • Too many breaches in one shift and the terminal is evacuated. You repeat the shift. A supervisor allows more.
  • Every shift has an objective. Complete it for a cash bonus.
  • Ceramic knives and 3D-printed guns show up green only, so the metal gate will not warn you.
  • Run out of time and the bag goes through unchecked.
